Documenting Foodways

CULINDEX maps hyper-regional culinary diversity across southern India, focusing on domestic cooking, trade influences, and agricultural context.

Why this archive matters

Beyond restaurant cuisine, this project records the foods people actually cook, preserve, adapt, and inherit across generations.

From the spice-heavy Mappila kitchens of the Malabar coast to the sun-dried, layered flavors of Chettinad, the archive treats food as cultural memory.

What the project tracks

  • Household recipes and seasonal variations
  • Ingredient origins and local produce systems
  • Community-specific cooking practices
  • Historical trade and migration influences

Vernacular Landscapes

Categorizing cuisines by micro-regions, communities, and historical trade routes.

Kerala

Documenting the nuances of Syrian Christian, Mappila, and Namboothiri foodways, tracing the historical impact of the Spice Route from Kasaragod to Thiruvananthapuram.

Tamil Nadu

Cataloging the distinct regional preparations of Kongunadu, Chettinad, Madurai, and Nanjilnadu, with attention to agricultural ties and indigenous ingredients.
