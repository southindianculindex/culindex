The Culinary Heritage Index

An evolving cultural map documenting the hyper-regional vernacular cuisines, agricultural ties, and trade histories of Kerala and Tamil Nadu.

Explore the Archive

Documenting Foodways

Moving beyond restaurant menus to record the foods people actually cook, preserve, adapt, and inherit across generations in Southern India.

Project Scope

From the spice-heavy Mappila kitchens of the Malabar coast to the sun-dried, layered flavors of Chettinad, the archive treats food as cultural memory.

We are currently in Phase 1: Data Collection, compiling household recipes, seasonal variations, ingredient origins, and oral histories.

The Methodology

• Normalizing hyper-local ingredient terminology.

• Tracing historical trade and migration impacts.

• Linking local produce systems to geography.

• Preparing datasets for GIS spatial mapping.

Vernacular Landscapes

Categorizing cuisines by micro-regions, indigenous communities, and historical trade routes.

Kerala

Documenting the nuances of Syrian Christian, Mappila, Tribal, and Namboothiri foodways, tracing the historical impact of the Spice Route from Kasaragod to Thiruvananthapuram.

Tamil Nadu

Cataloging the distinct regional preparations of Kongunadu, Chettinad, Madurai, and Nanjilnadu, with attention to agricultural ties and indigenous ingredients.

Interactive Cultural Map

Our upcoming location-based module will allow researchers and enthusiasts to explore recipes, ingredients, and culinary histories by exact geographic coordinates.
