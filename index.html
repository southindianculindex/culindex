About the Project

CULINDEX documents domestic recipes, indigenous ingredients, oral histories, agricultural traditions and regional culinary practices to build an open scholarly archive of South Indian food heritage.

Research Regions

Kerala

Syrian Christian, Mappila, Tribal, Namboothiri and regional cuisines across the state.

Tamil Nadu

Chettinad, Kongunadu, Madurai, Nanjil Nadu and other vernacular food traditions.
